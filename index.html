      Questions

      Before you get in touch

      Do I need to visit your office?

      No. Most clients share documents by email or WhatsApp and we speak on the phone or a video call. You're welcome to meet us at our Coimbatore office.

      Is a Virtual CFO only for large companies?

      No. Monthly MIS and a weekly cash forecast are most useful for owner-run businesses and startups that don't have a full-time finance head.

      I've received a GST or Income Tax notice. What should I send?

      The notice, the returns for the period it covers, and your books or bank statements for that period. Note the reply deadline and contact us as early as you can.

      Who can file an MSEFC claim?

      A micro or small enterprise registered on Udyam that supplied goods or services and has not been paid within the time allowed under the MSMED Act can claim the amount with interest before the MSE Facilitation Council.

      Can you take over from my current accountant?

      Yes. We collect your past returns and portal access, review them for open issues, and give you a list of anything pending before our first filing.

      Do you work with clients outside Coimbatore?

      Yes. Returns, ROC filings, MIS and faceless income tax assessments are all handled online for clients anywhere in India.
